Moli binaries with screencast video support statically link x264, distributed
under GPL-2.0-or-later. This applies to the linked binary even when recording
is not enabled at runtime. The MIT / Apache-2.0 notices describe Moli's own
source and other dependencies, and do not replace x264's license.

The exact x264 source, GPL text, NASM source/build-tool license, bridge and
build instructions are included in sources/moli-screencast-video. Revisions
and hashes are in native/README.md. The full Moli source, Cargo.lock and build
configuration are at https://github.com/lexmount/moli under the release tag
matching VERSION in this package. Cargo dependencies retain their licenses.
